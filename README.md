# pegrna-structural-bioinf
This is my master’s thesis project. In this project, I describe our work and ideas related to the generation of artificial prime-editing guide RNAs (pegRNAs) by chemical ligation.

Our aim is to obtain artificially modified pegRNAs by chemically ligating three or four short ribooligonucleotides and to compare their efficiency with that of unmodified pegRNAs assembled by enzymatic ligation using T4 RNA ligase.
We plan to synthesize six different short modified ribooligonucleotides using automated solid-phase synthesis:

PegRNA31 is equale PegRNA41 (protospacer sequence): 5'-ACAGGGUGUGGCAGAAGCAGCGUUUUAGAGCUAGA-azide (35 n.b.)

PegRNA42: 5'-alkyne-AAUAGCAAGUUAAAAUAAGGCUAGUCCGUUAUCAACUUGAAA-NH2 (42 n.b.)

PegRNA43: 5'-alkyne-AAGUGGCACCGAGUCGGUGCGUCUUUCCUGGUGCUUCUGCCACA-NH2 (44 n.b.)

PegRNA44: 5'-alkyne-CGCGGUUCUAUCUAGUUACGCGUUAAACCAACUAGAA (37 n.b.)

PegRNA32: 5'-alkyne-AAUAGCAAGUUAAAAUAAGGCUAGUCCGUUAUCAACUUGAAAAAGUGGCACCGAGUCGGUGC-NH2 (62 n.b.)

PegRNA33: 5'-alkyne-GUCUUUCCUGGUGCUUCUGCCACACGCGGUUCUAUCUAGUUACGCGUUAAACCAACUAGAA (61 n.b.)

We will construct pegRNAs containing artificial linkers by chemical ligation. First, pegRNA41 will be ligated to pegRNA42 via azide–alkyne cycloaddition. The resulting product will then be reacted with azidobutanoic acid. Next, it will be ligated to pegRNA43 via azide–alkyne cycloaddition, followed by another reaction with azidobutanoic acid. Finally, pegRNA44 will be added by chemical ligation.

We analyzed the three-dimensional structure of the prime-editing complex and the secondary structure of the pegRNA. We identified regions of the pegRNA that have relatively few interactions with the protein and used these regions to design the positions of the artificial linkers in the ligated pegRNA.

However, I believe that it is important to use modern structural bioinformatics tools to analyze more accurately the possible structural changes caused by the introduction of artificial linkers. Therefore, I began studying structural bioinformatics independently.

My goal is to perform molecular dynamics simulations of the prime-editing complex containing the modified pegRNA within the next ten months.
